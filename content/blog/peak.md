# Peak: Gaming Doesn't Have to be Complicated

[Peak](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK) is a four player co-op game about scouts (like the Boy Scouts or Girl Scouts) being stranded on a deserted island after a plane crash. You must climb to the *peak* of the island in order to be rescued. The main focus of Peak is the physics. Your character's limbs flail around as you climb, jump, and run through the various biomes that make up the island. You can pick up and throw items, use ropes to help your friends, and find plenty of other interactable items.
